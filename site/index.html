Let’s talk
Is Wavelink only for inventory and manifests?

No. Inventory and manifests are important parts of a wider operations platform. Wavelink also brings together maintenance, certificates, tasks, handovers, checklists, toolbox talks, fault and safety records, vessel logs, planning and access management. A walkthrough can focus on the modules your company needs.

How are inventory and manifests connected?

Manifest contents stay linked to equipment and box records. Teams prepare and dispatch the shipment, confirm receipt, then record final placement. The equipment keeps its identity and movement history rather than becoming a disconnected new inventory item when it reaches the vessel.

Can we manage boxes, stock levels and equipment records?

Inventory covers equipment, consumables and boxes with contents, organised by location and department. QR verification supports stock checks; quantity information and low-stock alerts help teams review supplies. Equipment can keep its certificate files, maintenance records and recorded movement history. These are recorded locations, not continuous GPS tracking.

How does Wavelink help teams work together?

Teams contribute to the same operational context through their own responsibilities: logistics handles stock and shipments; crews complete checks and logs; technicians review equipment records; supervisors review work and handovers. Access is configured by role and supported module controls. This is not live chat, simultaneous editing or automatic synchronisation between separate installations.

Can we use company email and two-step verification?

Company-email sign-in and two-step verification are planned for future releases; their availability must be confirmed for your deployment. The public demo should not be treated as a deployment with those protections. Corporate single sign-on (SSO), identity-provider support and rollout policies are separate requirements to discuss with your IT team; no provider integration is promised here.

What security assurances can we review?

Discuss the intended deployment, user access, hosting, data handling and backup requirements with us before rollout. Access controls are part of the product scope, but no independent security audit, ISO 27001 or SOC 2 certification, encryption-at-rest standard, uptime guarantee or regulatory-compliance certification is claimed by this website.

Are these actual application screenshots?

The product views are readable illustrations with fictional people, equipment and records. They explain capabilities, rather than reproduce the exact current interface. Open the separate Wavelink demo to see the application itself. Do not enter real company or operational data into a public demo.

How do we discuss deployment and pricing?

Start with a walkthrough. Tell us about your vessels or sites, team size, local or hosted requirements and the records you need to manage. We can then discuss the appropriate scope, availability, security requirements and commercial arrangements for your project.
