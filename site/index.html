What can Wavelink bring together?+

Inventory, boxes, manifests and receiving; maintenance, certificates and faults; tasks, logs and calendar; checklists, toolbox talks and HSE / QSHE; daily and hitch handovers; profiles, Team, Original Files and company access. The walkthrough can focus on the workflows your operation needs.

Can we import our current Word, PDF and Excel documents?+

Supported files can be extracted into editable proposals for inventory, checklist and routine definitions, toolbox forms or logbook layouts. Visible Excel worksheets and tables are named separately. Choose one, map or edit the contents, then review and confirm a separate draft or list. Extraction does not decipher every layout; scanned files need clear originals and configured OCR. Existing publications, signatures and operational records are not inferred from the source.

How does QR signing work?+

An authorised organiser can issue a short-lived QR or link to one saved toolbox talk or one exact published handover. Named members sign with their own identity; temporary visitors can participate when explicitly permitted. Saved names, acknowledgement wording, timestamps and optional drawn signatures stay with that revision. Visitor identity is self-declared, and signing does not grant access to the rest of the company workspace. QR signing requires HTTPS and a connection to the service.

Can we work without internet on a vessel?+

A local Windows hub can serve phones, tablets and laptops on the vessel network without an internet connection for that workspace. Those devices still need a connection to the hub. Hosted access requires a connection to the hosted service. Automatic synchronisation between separate local and hosted installations is not part of this offer.

Does Wavelink support company email and two-step verification?+

The current application supports sign-in with an exact verified membership email inside the selected company service, alongside User ID sign-in. Members can enrol authenticator-based two-step verification, keep recovery codes and review or revoke their sessions. Invitations, mail delivery and deployment controls need company setup. Public email-based company discovery and enterprise SSO are separate requirements.

What do profiles, points and badges mean?+

Company member profiles and authorised Team updates help make contributions visible. Qualifying recorded work can earn contribution points and unlock badge choices. Badges are recognition only: they are not qualifications and never grant permissions, competency or approval authority.

Are the HR packages available now?+

The HR packages shown here are planned additions. Their scope, release dates and commercial terms have not yet been confirmed. Current department handover shifts support daily operational continuity; personnel rotations, timesheets and expense claims are separate proposed packages. Contact us to discuss priorities for your company.
