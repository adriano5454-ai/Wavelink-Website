Wavelink / Fleet operations guide

Offshore fleet operations

See what needs attention before the next operation, where each vessel last reported, and how much freshwater or fuel it used.

Review operational readiness

See overdue maintenance, equipment or crew document expiries, and outstanding safety and quality items in one view. The charts group findings by source and respect what the signed-in account can see. This view supports review; it is not a clearance decision or a compliance rating.

See the last reported vessel positions

Use the fleet map to see the last position reported for each vessel and when it was recorded. Older positions stay marked as stale, and vessels without a report stay visible in the list. Positions are entered manually. This is not live AIS or a navigation chart.

Separate tank levels from consumption

Use Water & Fuel to compare manual tank readings over time. In the Consumption tab, enter opening and closing stock for the vessel and note refills, onboard freshwater production and external transfers. The daily chart shows estimated usage separately for each product. An administrator can limit posting access by vessel.

Try it with fictional data

Open Fleet & vessels, choose Water & Fuel, select a vessel and open Consumption. Use Try adding an entry to preview the estimate. The practice entry is not saved, and the public demo does not take real operational data.
