WEBSITE INFORMATION

Privacy & this website.

Information about the Wavelink marketing website at www.mywavelink.com.

Website release 2.3.1 · 25 September 2026

What this page does

This is a static marketing website for Wavelink by AJ Offshore Solutions. The published website code does not include analytics, advertising trackers, account registration, contact forms, tracking cookies or browser storage. Its interactive examples run in your browser and do not send example selections to us.

Your hosting provider may process technical request information, such as IP addresses and browser request data, as part of serving and protecting the site. Hosting behaviour is separate from the website code and depends on the provider and its configuration.

Contact by email

Contact links open your email application. Email is sent only when you choose to send it. Information you include in an enquiry will be handled through the recipient’s email service to respond to that enquiry. Please do not send passwords, sensitive client information or confidential operational records in an initial enquiry.

The copy-email button only attempts to copy the visible email address after you click it. It does not read your clipboard.

The separate Wavelink demo

The demo at demo.mywavelink.com (opens in a new tab) is a separate application. It may have its own sessions, records, cookies and hosting behaviour. This page describes the marketing website, not the demo application or a customer deployment. Do not enter sensitive or real operational data into the public demo.

Illustrations and product information

The website’s interface previews, equipment names, vessel names and example records are fictional illustrations. They are not exact application screenshots or customer records. The offshore image is illustrative imagery supplied with the website, not a photograph offered as evidence of a customer operation.

Preview selections change illustrations only. They do not create records, trigger application actions or update the demo. Discuss deployment, permissions and the capabilities you need before using Wavelink for a live project.

Security roadmap information

Company-email sign-in and two-step verification are described as planned product features. They are not implemented by this marketing website, and their availability and configuration must be confirmed for a customer deployment. Company-email access does not itself establish corporate single sign-on or a particular identity-provider integration. No independent security certification is claimed.
