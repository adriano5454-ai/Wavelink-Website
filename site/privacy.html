Privacy and website information

Information about the Wavelink marketing website at www.mywavelink.com.

Website release 3.3.2 · 17 November 2026

Website code and hosting

This is a static marketing website. Its code has no analytics, advertising trackers, contact forms, account registration, tracking cookies or browser storage. The interactive product examples use fictional records and run in your browser; example selections are not sent to us.

The hosting provider may process technical information such as IP addresses and browser request data when serving and protecting the site. Provider behaviour depends on the deployment and is separate from the website code.

Contact by email

Contact links open your email application. Information is sent only when you choose to send an email. The recipient’s email service handles your enquiry so the team can respond. Keep passwords and confidential operational records out of an initial enquiry.

The copy-email control attempts to write the displayed address to your clipboard after a click. It does not read your clipboard.

Separate demo and company application

The Wavelink demo (opens in a new tab) is a separate application with its own records, sessions and hosting behaviour. Explore it with fictional information. Company deployments have their own access, data and operational requirements.

The document import example on this website accepts no files. In the company application, standard import reads documents on the Wavelink hub and retains selected source metadata. Source bytes from this import are temporary unless you also upload the document to Original Files.

An employee ticket, certificate or receipt attached to an HR record is stored with that record and is accessible according to company permissions.

Optional OpenAI assistance in the company application

Inventory mapping sends the selected column headers, up to eight sample rows and the current column choices when you confirm the preview.

With OpenAI configured in Wavelink 1.34.44, authorised users can also send up to 6,000 characters from an import, a template brief, shift notes or an HR document. The request identifies the form type and language so OpenAI can suggest the appropriate fields. The app shows you the text to review and edit before you send it.

For HR, Wavelink can read the text from a selected PDF, JPEG or PNG on the company hub. Remove details you do not need for the form, such as identity numbers or medical findings. The OpenAI request contains the reviewed text. The attachment stays on the hub when saved. You can also paste a short excerpt yourself.

You review the suggestions, apply the fields you choose, then save through the usual workflow. Manual editing remains available.

Quais dados são enviados à OpenAI?

No inventário, você confere os cabeçalhos, até oito linhas de exemplo e as colunas selecionadas antes do envio. No Wavelink 1.34.44, quando a integração estiver configurada, também pode revisar e editar um trecho de até 6.000 caracteres para pedir sugestões de campos.

No RH, a leitura da passagem, do certificado ou do recibo ocorre no servidor da empresa. Antes de enviar o trecho à OpenAI, remova dados pessoais desnecessários. O pedido inclui o texto revisado, o tipo de formulário e o idioma. Depois, você escolhe quais sugestões aplicar e revisa o registro antes de salvar.

Illustrations and product information

Website interface examples, equipment, companies, vessels and people are fictional. They illustrate capabilities and are not exact application screenshots or customer records. The supplied offshore image is illustrative, not evidence of a customer operation. Exploring an example does not create, sign, publish or change an application record.

The HR section describes implemented personal documents, travel, crew assignments, weekly project timesheets and expense records. Its crew view uses fictional people and dates. The installed version, company permissions and deployment configuration determine access to these tools.

The HR content was checked against application UI139 and its retained UI117 and UI129 feature records. Confirm the installed version, supported workflow and deployment configuration during your company evaluation.

Account controls

The current application includes exact verified membership-email sign-in within a selected company, optional authenticator two-step verification, recovery codes and session management. These controls are implemented in the application, not by this marketing website, and require appropriate company setup. Enterprise SSO and public email-based company discovery are separate requirements. No independent security certification is claimed here.
