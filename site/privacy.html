WEBSITE INFORMATION

Privacy & this website.

Information about the Wavelink marketing website at www.mywavelink.com.

Website release 3.0.1 · 4 October 2026

Website code and hosting

This is a static marketing website. Its code has no analytics, advertising trackers, contact forms, account registration, tracking cookies or browser storage. The interactive product examples use fictional records and run in your browser; example selections are not sent to us.

The hosting provider may process technical information such as IP addresses and browser request data when serving and protecting the site. Provider behaviour depends on the deployment and is separate from the website code.

Contact by email

Contact links open your email application. Information is sent only when you choose to send an email. The recipient’s email service handles your enquiry so the team can respond. Keep passwords and confidential operational records out of an initial enquiry.

The copy-email control attempts to write the displayed address to your clipboard after a click. It does not read your clipboard.

Separate demo and company application

The Wavelink demo (opens in a new tab) is a separate application with its own records, sessions and hosting behaviour. Explore it with fictional information. Company deployments have their own access, data and operational requirements.

The document import example on this website accepts no files. The actual application supports reviewed document extraction. In the current implementation, source extraction runs on the Wavelink hub without sending documents to an external extraction service. Selected source metadata is retained; original bytes are temporary unless the user separately uploads the source to Original Files.

Illustrations and product information

Website interface examples, equipment, companies, vessels and people are fictional. They illustrate capabilities and are not exact application screenshots or customer records. The supplied offshore image is illustrative, not evidence of a customer operation. Exploring an example does not create, sign, publish or change an application record.

Content was checked against the current UI95 application release. Confirm the installed version, supported workflow and deployment configuration during your company evaluation.

Account controls

The current application includes exact verified membership-email sign-in within a selected company, optional authenticator two-step verification, recovery codes and session management. These controls are implemented in the application, not by this marketing website, and require appropriate company setup. Enterprise SSO and public email-based company discovery are separate requirements. No independent security certification is claimed here.
