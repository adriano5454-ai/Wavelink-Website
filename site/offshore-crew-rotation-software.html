Wavelink / Workflow guide

Offshore crew rotation and roster software

Know who is on board, who is ashore and who is due to join next. Wavelink helps offshore coordinators plan relief crews and keep the roster aligned with each vessel or shore base.

Review the roster by vessel or base

Start with the location you are planning for. Review crew assignments together instead of opening employee records one at a time. Use the joining and leaving dates to see when the outgoing and incoming crews overlap. A person listed as ashore is not necessarily available for the next trip; check planned travel and leave before committing them.

Keep shifts and rotations separate

A rotation describes the time someone is assigned to the vessel or base. A shift window records when they are due to work within that assignment. Record both when planning a relief crew so a joining date is not mistaken for the start of a particular watch. Add a project number when you need to identify the assignment in later reviews.

Prepare for the next crew change

Use the upcoming assignments to confirm the relief crew, then use the HR workspace to review travel details and check document expiry dates where your role allows. Record assignment changes in the roster and keep operational follow-up for the incoming watch in the shift handover.

Example: a staggered relief crew

One technician joins the day before another leaves, and the relief surveyor arrives on the following day. In the roster, review the dates against each vessel assignment and check the shift windows. The overlap gives the technicians a window to hand over equipment tasks, while the survey team can prepare a separate handover for its later change.
